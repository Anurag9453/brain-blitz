Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

Summary for Students & Parents: MathBlitz is an educational, curriculum-aligned mental mathematics and general knowledge learning game for students (Classes 6–10). We do not collect personal identifiers from young players to play the game. All gameplay progress can be played anonymously. We do not sell player data or track physical location.

1. Introduction & Scope

MathBlitz ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting the personal information of players, students, and parents. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards concerning the collection, storage, and protection of information when you use the MathBlitz mobile application.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K & India DPDP Act Compliance)

MathBlitz is designed for general audiences, specifically school students aged 11 and older:

  • No Required Personal Data: Players are not required to provide real names, phone numbers, addresses, or photos. Players start on a random anonymous device session and choose an arbitrary in-game nickname.
  • Parental Rights: Parents or legal guardians may at any time request to review, update, or permanently delete their child's account data by emailing us at support@mathblitz.app.
  • No Location Tracking: MathBlitz does not collect or store precise GPS or geolocation data.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate game services and rankings:

  • Gameplay Records: Scores, accuracy percentage, time elapsed, stars unlocked, XP level, and league tier rankings.
  • Anonymous Identifier: A randomly generated UUID created by our secure database to identify your device session.
  • Optional Account Data: If a player or parent chooses to link their account to prevent progress loss, we store the provided email address and a cryptographically salted password hash.
  • Transaction Data: If in-app virtual coin packs are purchased, our payment provider (Razorpay) provides an order ID, payment reference ID, and timestamp. MathBlitz never sees, receives, or stores payment card numbers, bank credentials, or UPI PINs. All payments are processed securely by PCI-DSS certified payment processors.

4. How We Use Information

Information is used exclusively for:

  • Synchronizing your stars, coins, and level progress across sessions.
  • Matching you in weekly league leaderboards and private Play-with-Friends rooms.
  • Preventing automated botting and cheating to ensure fair competition.
  • Providing customer and educational support.

5. In-Game Virtual Currency (Non-Gambling Disclosure)

MathBlitz coins (₵) are purely virtual points for unlocking higher difficulty levels and cosmetic visual color themes within the application. Coins have no real-world monetary value, cannot be redeemed or cashed out for fiat currency or physical prizes, and are non-transferable between players outside the game mechanics.

6. Advertisements

Any optional rewarded ads (e.g., watching a brief video to earn virtual coins) are non-personalized, contextual, and filtered strictly for family-safe, child-appropriate educational audiences in compliance with Google Play Families Policy and Apple App Store Review Guidelines.

7. Data Security & Retention

All network communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS 1.3). Database tables are enforced with Row Level Security (RLS) restricting access strictly to the authenticated account holder.

8. Data Deletion Requests & Account Deletion

You have the absolute right to delete your profile and all associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account directly inside the app at any time. Tap your profile pill on the Home screen to open Account Settings, scroll to Delete Account, and tap Permanently Delete My Account. This immediately and irreversibly wipes your profile, gameplay history, ratings, and authentication credentials.
  • Email Deletion Request: If you are unable to access the application, you or your parent/guardian may request account deletion by emailing support@mathblitz.app with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Requests are verified and completed within 7 business days.
  • Financial Record Retention: In compliance with applicable statutory taxation and company financial accounting regulations, basic transactional receipts (order ID, amount paid, and timestamp) are retained in an anonymized state (with your user identity permanently detached and unlinked).
